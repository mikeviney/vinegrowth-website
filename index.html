About Vine Growth

We know every angle of this industry - because we've worked them all

Vine Growth is a hands-on consultancy founded by an operator with over a decade of building and growing businesses in the luxury watch and jewellery sector. We know what it means to start from zero, win the first client, hit the growth ceiling, and find a way through it.

Our experience covers the full commercial landscape of the industry - sales, marketing, technology, wholesale, trade media, and auctions. It is rare to find that depth of working knowledge across so many disciplines within a single sector - and rarer still in one person.

At any one time, Vine Growth works with a deliberately small number of clients - because full attention and genuine commitment are non-negotiable.
